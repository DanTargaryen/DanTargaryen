### About me

I'm **Ming Lin (林明)**, an undergraduate student.

I work on **video generation and world models**, focusing on memory for long-horizon generation and efficient distillation of diffusion models.

### Research interests

- **Video generation** · Video diffusion models and controllable generation.
- **World models & memory** · Memory-augmented generation and long-horizon consistency.
- **Efficient generation** · Diffusion model distillation and accelerated inference.
