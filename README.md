# Ming Lin / 林明

Undergraduate student in the **School of Software, Beihang University**.

I work on video generation and world models, with a focus on memory mechanisms for long-horizon generation and efficient distillation of diffusion models.

## Research Interests

- **Video generation:** video diffusion models and controllable generation.
- **World models and memory:** memory-augmented video generation and long-horizon consistency.
- **Efficient generation:** diffusion model distillation and accelerated inference.
